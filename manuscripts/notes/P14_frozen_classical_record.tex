\section{C3 repaired: the boundary identity and its orientation}
\noindent Builder/output: \texttt{boundary\_identity.py}, \texttt{P13\_boundary\_identity.json}.\par\smallskip
\textbf{Defect.} The p09/p10 control C3 evaluated the identity on $g=\eta+A(x^0)\,e_{00}$. This metric is locally
flat ($x^0\mapsto\int\sqrt{1+A}\,dx^0$ removes $A$); its only nonzero symbol is $\Gamma^0{}_{00}$, and
$\sqrt{-g}R=L_{\Gamma\Gamma}=B^\mu=\partial_\mu B^\mu=0$ identically. The check was therefore vacuous and could not
detect a sign; the source comment ``keeps $\partial B$ nontrivial'' was false. p09/p10 also stated the orientation as
$\sqrt{-g}R-L_{\Gamma\Gamma}=+\partial_\mu B^\mu$. That orientation is wrong.

\begin{proposition}[Boundary identity, corrected orientation]
With $B^\mu=\sqrt{-g}\,(g^{\mu\nu}\Gamma^\lambda_{\nu\lambda}-g^{\lambda\nu}\Gamma^\mu_{\lambda\nu})$,
\[\sqrt{-g}\,R-L_{\Gamma\Gamma}=-\,\partial_\mu B^\mu .\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Deterministic proof (three parts)]
(i) \emph{Orientation.} The statement above, with $-\partial_\mu B^\mu$, replaces the p09/p10 statement; it is used in
\texttt{conventions.BOUNDARY\_IDENTITY}, the exports and all certificates.
(ii) \emph{Nontrivial conformal control.} For $g=f\eta$ with $f$ a generic function of all four coordinates, sympy gives
exactly $\sqrt{-g}R=-3\Box f+\tfrac{3}{2f}(\partial f)^2$, $L_{\Gamma\Gamma}=\tfrac{3}{2f}(\partial f)^2$,
$B^\mu=3\partial^\mu f$. The identity holds with residual $0$; the p10 orientation leaves $-6\Box f\neq0$.
(iii) \emph{Generic jet proof.} Let $G^{ab}$ be ten \emph{free} symmetric symbols (the inverse metric), and let the
40 first jets $g_{ab,c}$ and 100 second jets $g_{ab,cd}$ be free. Use $\partial_cG^{ab}=-G^{ar}g_{rs,c}G^{sb}$ and
$\partial_c\sqrt{-g}=\tfrac12\sqrt{-g}\,G^{ab}g_{ab,c}$ (Jacobi; both checked exactly via $\partial\det=\mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{adj}\,\partial g)$
and $\mathrm{adj}\,g=\det g\,\mathbf 1$). Then $(\sqrt{-g}R-L_{\Gamma\Gamma}+\partial_\mu B^\mu)/\sqrt{-g}$ is a polynomial in
150 variables over $\mathbb Q$ (2406, 1764 and 2442 terms in the three pieces) and is \emph{identically zero} for all ten
components, first and second jets; with the p10 orientation it has 2736 nonzero terms. Because the identity holds for
free $G$, it holds a fortiori for $G=g^{-1}$.
\end{proof}
\textbf{Integrated-action equivalence is unaffected.} For compactly supported $h$, $\int\partial_\mu B^\mu=0$ with either
sign, so $S_{\rm EH}=-(2/\kappa^2)\int L_{\Gamma\Gamma}$ and $\ell_k=-2[\varepsilon^k]L_{\Gamma\Gamma}$ are unchanged. The
error concerned the stated local identity, not the action.

\begin{lemma}[One-loop enumeration, \texttt{max\_len}=2]
(i) A connected one-loop graph is 1PI iff every internal line lies on its unique cycle, since a line off the cycle is a
bridge. So after the quadratic ($\kappa^0$, two-field) terms are absorbed into propagators, a one-loop 1PI graph is a
cycle of interaction vertices, each with exactly two internal legs.
(ii) Every retained interaction vertex has coupling order $\kappa^n$ with $n\ge1$. Mechanically, the $\kappa^0$
inventory contains only two-field monomials.
(iii) Hence a $\kappa^{\le2}$ contribution has at most two vertices, and \texttt{max\_len}=2 is exhaustive.
(iv) The auxiliary $b$ occurs only in $\kappa^0$ bilinears ($b^2$, $bh$, $b\bar c^*$; checked). Its Gaussian elimination
produces only bilinear (kinetic or source-bilinear) terms, never an omitted interaction.
(v) Source-linear monomials (one quantum leg) cannot sit on the cycle. Attached off it, they create a bridge, i.e.\ a
one-particle-reducible graph, so they cannot create extra 1PI coefficients.
\end{lemma}
\noindent The relation $\Gamma_\Psi\leftrightarrow\Gamma$ used to read off rows is the defined classical
pullback/interface above, not a quantum gauge-change theorem.
