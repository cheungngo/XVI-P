\paragraph{Erratum K1 (the p19 text is retracted).} p19 stated that full $\sigma_0$ closure of the MS-bar counterterm
fails with the derived ghost-equation factor $+1$, that it needs $-1$, and that this was unresolved. The statement is
wrong; it came from a Grassmann-orientation bookkeeping error.
\begin{itemize}
\item The route-G densities are generated in the order (c,cb). The P15 ghost self-energy coordinates $\Sigma$ use the
  (cb,c) orientation, and $V[\mathrm{c.cb}\#k]=-V[(\mathrm{cb,c})\#k]$. p20 measures this ratio (via
  \texttt{pw\_vertex}) as $-1$ for $k=0,1$.
\item In route-G (c,cb) coordinates, $\Sigma$ has pole $(+3/8,-1/4)$ and local part $(2/3,-5/12)$.
\item The derived factor is $+1$ (the ghost equation $\Sigma=2iD[G_{Kc}]$ reproduces the P15 pole). With it, the
  \emph{complete} MS-bar counterterm $S_{ct}=-(\text{h.h}+\text{c.K}+\text{c.cb poles})$ is $\sigma_0$-closed in
  every ghost $+1$ sector (h.c, c.b, c.cbs).
\item Without the c.cb term, or with $\Sigma$ unconverted (the p19 error), $\sigma_0(S_{ct})$ has 88 entries in the
  c.b sector.
\item Tree-level controls: $V_{S_0}[\mathrm{cb,c}]=2iD\,V_{S_0}[K,c]$, and $\sigma_0(S_0^{Kc}+S_0^{\bar cc})=0$ (the
  $S_0^{Kc}$ part alone has a nonzero image).
\item In quotient coordinates, the c.K-only data have c.cb components $(-3/8,1/4)$, which cancel against
  $\Sigma=(+3/8,-1/4)$. The full data including $\Sigma$ have quotient coordinates $0$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Restoration redefined.} The p19 flag (renormalised $P_{ch}\sigma_0(S_{ct})=0$, $x=0$) follows from the
identity and locality, so it is not independent. In p20, \texttt{projected\_restoration\_verified} means full
$\sigma_0$ closure of the complete one-loop $\kappa^2$ MS-bar $S_{ct}$ (including c.cb, $\Sigma$ held fixed) in all
ghost $+1$ sectors, together with the projected identity. Scope: that counterterm only; not bulk symmetry, not O.1.
The corrected computation closes, so the flag is earned. The p19 check is kept as a labelled route-consistency
control, and it holds.

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Derived from package data.} The 4D UV pole of D11 (\texttt{P18\_D11.json}, package orbit convention;
  transverse; only spin-2 and spin-0s parts) has Barnes--Rivers coefficients $c_2=-i/240$ and $c_0=-i/24$, so
  $c_0:c_2=10$. The quadratic forms on $g=\eta+h$ are derived here from linearised curvature: $C^2\to\frac12
  p^4hP_2h$, $R^2\to3p^4hP_0^sh$, and Gauss--Bonnet $\to0$.

  $\frac1{120}C^2+\frac1{72}R^2$ (Birrell--Davies \S6.2; 't~Hooft--Veltman 1974). This predicts
  $\frac1{240}:\frac1{24}=1:10$.
