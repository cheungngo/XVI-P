\paragraph{Absolute D11 pin (p20: OPEN).} The sign and magnitude of the D11 UV pole are derived from
$\tfrac12\mathrm{Tr}\ln$ of the minimal scalar loop under the package's declared conventions, each checked verbatim
in the code:
\begin{itemize}
\item $G=\Gamma^{(2)}$: the amplitude rules declare $i\Gamma_1=\int\mathrm{pref}\,N/\prod(q^2+i0)$ and G6 declares
  $G=\kappa^2N_\epsilon\hat G$, $\hat G=-i\,\mathrm{pref}/\kappa^2\int N/(D_1D_2)$; hence $G=-i\cdot i\Gamma=\Gamma$.
\item Wick direction: $N_\epsilon=iS_\epsilon/(16\pi^2)$ and the certified bubble pole $\hat I(1,1)=+1/\epsilon$ give
  $d^dl_M=+i\,d^dl_E$; with $\eta=(+,-,-,-)$ and $S_\phi=\tfrac12\int\sqrt{-g}\,g^{\mu\nu}\partial\phi\partial\phi$
  this gives $iS_M=-S_E$, so $L^{\rm eff}_M=-L^{\rm eff}_E$.
\item Heat kernel (Euclidean, $d=4-2\epsilon$): $L_{E,\rm div}=-a_2/(32\pi^2\epsilon)$, so
  $L_{M,\rm div}=+a_2/(32\pi^2\epsilon)$ with $a_2=C^2/120+R^2/72$ ($\xi=0$).
\item With $g=\eta+\kappa h$ the Minkowski quadratic forms are $C^2\to\tfrac12p^4hP_2h$ and $R^2\to3p^4hP_0h$.
\end{itemize}
Result: $\Gamma^{(2)}|_{\rm pole}=\kappa^2/(16\pi^2\epsilon)\,[p^4P_2/240+p^4P_0/24]$. This equals the package pole
$\kappa^2N_\epsilon\hat G/\epsilon$ with $c_2=-i/240$, $c_0=-i/24$ exactly (ratio 1 for both spins). No convention
choice is external; the external inputs are mathematics only (the $a_2$ coefficients and the heat-kernel pole
formula). A $\times2$ normalisation error and a sign flip keep the 10:1 ratio but are detected by the absolute pin.
